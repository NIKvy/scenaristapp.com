How the page reads what you type

These are the Fountain conventions — the plain-text screenplay format that Scenarist, Final Draft, Highland and Slugline all read.

INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT
A line that starts with INT., EXT. or I/E is a scene heading.
MAYA
A name in capitals after an empty line is a character.
Where were you?
The lines right under a character are that character’s dialogue.
(quietly)
Parentheses on their own line, inside dialogue, are a parenthetical.
CUT TO:
Capitals ending in TO: are a transition, set to the right edge.
↵ ↵
An empty line ends the dialogue. Whatever comes next is action again.

In the app none of this is on you. Scenarist formats as you type, capitalises names and headings, and Smart Keys and autocomplete fill in the cast, the locations and the (V.O.).
